# Notes for Claude

- Engine: Godot 4.3 stable. Everything is 2D (`gl_compatibility`, `variant/thread_support=false`, 640x360 viewport scaled with integer `canvas_items` stretch). Don't use features that only work in Forward+.
- Art style follows "The Hunger Beneath Us": flat top-down LPC Revised 32px pixel art, layered characters, 96px portraits in the dialogue box. Keep `texture_filter` nearest and everything on whole pixels.
- Fights: `scripts/battle/battle_rules.gd` (static, seedable through `b["_rng"]`) holds the rules and `scripts/battle/battle.gd` draws them over the town; foes with moves, tells and phases are `data/foes.json`, art at `assets/foes/<id>.png` (two 96px frames, painted by `tools/art_creatures.py`, which `tools/art_foes.py` calls). Foes of kind `anomaly` get the legendary entrance in `battle.gd` (`_reveal_anomaly`: dark, silhouette, flash, name card, sting), `uncanny` a static hit; the sounds are synthesised by `tools/make_audio.py` into `assets/audio`. A prop or NPC with `"fight"` starts one, a map's `encounters` roll random ones at night, an anomaly is observed (photograph, record, read against its `tells`) rather than beaten, and `"use": "siege"` runs the case's `siege` table (`scripts/battle/siege.gd`). Debug `?fight=<foe,foe>`.
- Seasons: Point Pleasant is winter (`ground` snow, `weather` snow), Sandown is summer (`tools/art_summer.py` holds the summer tiles, seaside props, island facades and people; maps say `"weather": "clear" | "mist" | "rain" | "gale"`). Sandown's clock is the tide (`tide` on the case, `{"tide": "low"}` gates), its lair appears on a `{"signs": 3}` gate (`appears` on a facade), and its road leg changes `mode` per stop (plane, train, hovercraft). Kelly is late summer Kentucky (`tools/art_kelly.py`), four days long, and its gates count days (`{"day": 3}`, `{"night": 5}`) toward the siege.
- Art is generated, not hand-placed: `tools/build_art.py` (Pillow) composes `assets/tiles`, `assets/props`, `assets/facades`, `assets/characters` and `assets/portraits` from the LPC Revised sheets kept in `assets/lpc_src`. Run it with `LPC_SRC=<full LPC checkout>` (and `GEN_PORTRAITS=<dir of generated faces>`) after changing a recipe; commit the output. Every sheet used must be listed in `assets/CREDITS.md` (the script writes it).
- Content is data: cases, witnesses, evidence, gates and contradictions live in `data/cases/<case>.json` plus per-district fragments in `data/cases/<case>/*.json`; maps and interiors in `data/maps/*.json`; scripted conversations in `data/dialogue/<case>/*.json`; items in `data/items.json`. The schema is `docs/content-format.md`. `python3 tools/check_content.py` checks every reference without Godot; extend it and `GameData.validate()` together.
- Conversations: `scripts/town/script_talk.gd` runs the inner-voices scripts (passives, white and red checks, tones, fail-forward); witnesses without a script fall back to the quick facts menu in `witness_talk.gd`. Voice levels and checks are in `scripts/investigation.gd`.
- The game loop: a new game starts at the Sutton Office (`data/maps/sutton_office.json`, `data/office.json` for rooms, upgrades and budget), the station wagon drives a road leg (`data/road.json`, `scripts/road/road.gd`: stops, event cards, the radio dial), the case is worked in its districts, and the verdict is filed at the Office case board for budget. Shops are NPCs with `"shop"`; gear (tool, coat, two charms) lives in `GameState.gear` and feeds stats and voice levels through `Investigation.member_stat`.
- A district is one map file; `exits` walk the party to another map, `gates` in the case data lock exits, doors and barrier props. Day phases (morning, day, dusk, night) drive NPC `when` routines and trail signs with `night`.
- Speech checks and evidence rules are in `scripts/investigation.gd` (static, no scene access) so tests can drive them with forced rolls.
- Run `tests/run_tests.gd` headless before pushing; CI runs the same check and the web export.
- In game, M opens the case map (`scripts/town/map_talk.gd`): every story, area, room and road leg, from the title or town.
- Web debug: `?map=<map id>`, `?inside=<room>`, `?at=x,y` (tiles, room tiles when combined with `?inside`), `?time=0..1`, `?day=<n>`, `?flag=<a,b>`, `?level=<n>`, `?fight=<foe,foe>` and `?road=<leg id>` on the index URL jump the party around for screenshots. After adding a `class_name` script run `godot --headless --path . --import` or the tests will not see it.
- Design source of truth: the "Anomaly Files: JRPG Design Plan" doc, section "Systems v2" and "Season 1 build plan".
